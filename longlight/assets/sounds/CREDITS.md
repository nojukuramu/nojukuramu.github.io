# Sounds

Every recording here is public domain under CC0 1.0. All but one are from
[BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) (Joseph Sardin's library); the page turn is Kenney's.

Each was cut to the one event the game plays (the cut points are listed, in seconds into the
source file), mixed to mono, faded at its ends, levelled and encoded as a small MP3. The loops
(wind, flag, fires, sea, bubbles, radio static) are cut 1.5 s long and have that last 1.5 s
crossfaded over their first, so they never seam. Five files are Last Flame's, unchanged: its match
is the lighter's flame catching here, its page turn is a note being read, and three of its
footsteps on boards are the steps indoors.

Until a file has loaded the game plays a synthesised stand-in (`js/audio.js`). The rooms the huts
make of the wind, the muffling indoors, the radio's narrow band and the piano and cello's space
are made in `js/audio.js`, not recorded.
