# Models

Every model here is a [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) model, public domain under CC0 1.0. None
was made for this game; the station is furnished from what other people photographed and built.

Each is Poly Haven's 1k glTF, run through [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev)'s
`optimize`: geometry quantised, textures re-encoded as WebP at 256 or 512 px, and the mesh
simplified to a few thousand triangles. That is the packed set, the one the offline install keeps.

`../full/models/` holds the same models under the same names for the Full quality setting: not
simplified, textures at 1024 px. They are not in the offline install; the game fetches them the
first time Full is chosen, and keeps them after that.

The bookshelves are authored ten times too large and are scaled down in `js/places.js`. The huts,
their walls, roofs, stilts and doors, the beacons' tripods and towers, the flags, tents, sleds,
masts and lantern posts are built in code (`js/scenery.js`).
