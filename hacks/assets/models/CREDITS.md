# Models

Every model here is public domain (CC0 1.0), downloaded from OpenGameArt and converted for this
game: metres, the barrel along -Z, the grip at the origin, the sight line made level, and empty
nodes marking where the muzzle, the sight and the other hand go. The body's bind pose was baked
to unit-scale bones and its UVs moved onto an eight-band atlas the game paints for each player.
