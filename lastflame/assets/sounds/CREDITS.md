# Sounds

Every recording here is free to use: CC0 1.0 or public domain, as listed. Most are from
[BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) (Joseph Sardin's library, CC0), with Kenney's packs, a
few OpenGameArt contributors, and three field recordings from radio aporee and Wikimedia Commons.

Each was cut to the one event the game plays (the cut points are listed, in seconds into the
source file), mixed to mono, peak-normalised, faded at its ends and encoded as a small MP3. The
growls are pitched down 18%, the gasp is an inhale sped up and pitched up, the furniture drag is a
chair on tile pitched down and dulled toward wood. Until a file has loaded the game plays its own
synthesised version (`js/audio.js`), and the tiktik call is always synthesised.
