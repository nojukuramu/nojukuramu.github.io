# Models

Every model here is from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released into the public domain
under CC0 1.0 — no attribution required, credited anyway.

Each was downloaded as its 1k glTF and packed for this game with glTF-Transform: duplicates
merged, the heavy ones simplified (meshoptimizer), textures shrunk to 256 or 512 pixels and
re-encoded as WebP, tangents dropped (three derives them) and the geometry quantised
(KHR_mesh_quantization, which three's GLTFLoader reads without a decoder). Metres, y up, as
Poly Haven publishes them.
