        PUBs

        A ride is a room you are invited into. A PUB is the public road: turn it on and your name and position are shared with every rider nearby who has also turned it on, and you see theirs. It runs phone to phone like everything else here — nobody stores it — but it is public, and the people who can see it are strangers.

        Anyone nearby running RouteCast can see where you are, to about a hundred metres, for as long as this is on. Do not leave it on from home.
