# Music

Every sample here is from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), the
Versilian Community Sample Library, public domain under CC0 1.0. The upright piano was sampled
by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio in the winter of 2015–16; the library asks that he and Versilian
Studios be credited, and they are, here and in the game's About sheet.

There is no recorded music. These are single notes. The piano is sampled every major third, at
its softest dynamic, and the game plays the notes it wants by pitching the nearest sample at most
two semitones either way: a few notes now and then, more at a camp, at a beacon and at the end,
generated and never the same twice (`js/audio.js`). The cello section's long, soft notes hold a
drone under it once the nights come. The radio's tune, Shenandoah, is played on the same piano
and pushed through a radio's narrow band; the tune is older than anyone's copyright.

Each note is cut from the start of its sample (the piano to its first seven seconds), mixed to
mono, faded at its end and encoded as a small MP3. The file names are MIDI note numbers.
