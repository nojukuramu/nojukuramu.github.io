KaraokeNatin

One screen sings. Every phone is a remote.
Peer-to-peer over WebRTC — no account, no install, no server of ours.

Install KaraokeNatin Runs full-screen from your home screen or desktop, and opens without a browser bar.

Host

Open this on the TV, laptop, or whatever screen everyone can see.

Join

Scan the host's QR code, or type the six-character room code.

Library

Your saved songs and playlists, kept in this browser. Build a set list on the couch and queue the whole thing when the room opens — no room needed to browse it.

How does this work without a server?

Phones and the host screen talk directly to each other over a WebRTC data channel. The only thing two browsers cannot do alone is find each other in the first place, so the app borrows a public PeerJS broker as a meeting point: it passes along a few lines of connection details keyed by your room code, and then it is out of the loop. Your queue, your names, and your commands never touch it.

Song search goes through public Piped mirrors, falling back to Invidious if those are down. If every mirror is unreachable you can still paste a YouTube link — that path needs nobody's API.

Practical limits: the host tab has to stay open, and a very restrictive network (some corporate or campus Wi-Fi) can block peer-to-peer entirely.

    Disclaimer

    Video content

    KaraokeNatin does not host, store, upload, or stream any video. It embeds YouTube's own player, and every video plays from YouTube exactly as it would on their site. All content belongs to its respective owners, and your use of it is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube or Google.

    Third-party services

    Search results come from public Piped and Invidious mirrors, and rooms are paired through a public PeerJS broker. None of these are operated by this project. They are volunteer-run, they can be slow, change, or disappear without notice, and their availability is not something this app controls.

    Your data

    There is no account, no analytics, and no backend. Your library, name, and room details are stored in this browser's local storage and never leave your device unless you export them. Once a room is connected, song queues travel directly between your device and the other people in the room.

    Rooms are open to anyone with the code

    A room code is the only thing standing between your room and a stranger, and six characters is guessable given enough attempts. Treat a room as public: fine for a party, not for anything private. Close it when you are done.

    No warranty

    This is a hobby project provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Peer-to-peer connections can fail entirely on restrictive networks, and no guarantee is made that it will work in any particular setting.
