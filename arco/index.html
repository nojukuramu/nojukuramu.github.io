ARCO

A guitar for your phone. Six real strings, no samples.

  • Fret on the neck. With tap on, that is the note — a riff needs one hand.
  • Pick or strum on the body. The bridge end palm-mutes.
  • Slide along a string, bend across it, flick off to pull off.

For fullscreen on iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

On a desktop: A W S E D F T G Y H U J play the key's degrees, space strums, 1–6 pick a string, arrows move up the neck.
