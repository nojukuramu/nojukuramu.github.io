        Disclaimer

        Video content

        KaraokeNatin does not host, store, or stream any video. It embeds YouTube's own player, and every video plays from YouTube exactly as it would on their site. All content belongs to its respective owners, and your use of it is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube or Google.

        Third-party services

        Search results come from public Piped and Invidious mirrors, and rooms are paired through a public PeerJS broker. None of these are operated by this project. They are volunteer-run and can be slow, change, or disappear without notice.

        Your data

        No account, no analytics, no backend. Your library, name, and room details stay in this browser and never leave your device unless you export them. Once a room is connected, the queue travels directly between the devices in it.

        Rooms are open to anyone with the code

        A six-character code is the only thing between your room and a stranger. Treat a room as public: fine for a party, not for anything private. Close it when you are done.

        No warranty

        A hobby project, provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Peer-to-peer connections can fail entirely on restrictive networks.
